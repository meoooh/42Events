# Privacy Policy for 42 Events

Last updated: October 17, 2026

42 Events is a browser extension maintained by Han (meoooh). Its single purpose is to help existing 42 Intra users view event times in their local timezone and prepare events in Google Calendar.

## Information used on your device

The extension runs only on `https://profile.intra.42.fr/*` and `https://profile-v3.intra.42.fr/*`. It reads event information already available on those pages, such as the event title, description, start and end times, location, and event link. It uses your browser's timezone setting to display local times.

This information is processed in the page on your device. The extension does not send it to the developer, maintain a server, or store it in an extension database. It does not collect passwords, authentication tokens, payment information, browsing history outside the supported pages, or analytics. It does not use tracking cookies, advertising, or remotely hosted executable code.

## Google Calendar

When you choose a calendar button, the extension opens a `https://calendar.google.com/calendar/render` link. The link contains the selected event's title, dates, description, and location so that Google Calendar can prepare an event. These event details are sent to Google when you open that link. Google may also receive the normal connection information associated with visiting its website.

The extension does not authenticate with Google, read your calendars, or create or modify calendar entries automatically. You review the prepared event and decide whether to save it. Google's own privacy policy and account settings apply to your use of Google Calendar. 42's own policies apply to your use of 42 Intra.

## Sharing, retention, and your choices

The developer does not receive, sell, or retain your event data. The only event-data transfer initiated by the extension is the Google Calendar link you choose to open. Data is used only for the extension's stated purpose, never for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending decisions. The use of information by 42 Events complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can avoid sending event details to Google by not selecting a calendar button. You can disable or uninstall the extension at any time. Calendar entries you choose to save remain under your control in Google Calendar and are not removed by uninstalling the extension.

If you contact the developer through GitHub, GitHub processes the information you voluntarily submit under its own policies. GitHub issues are public; do not include passwords, tokens, or private event details.

## Contact and changes

Questions can be submitted at <https://github.com/meoooh/42Events/issues>. Updates to this policy will be published in this repository with a revised date.
