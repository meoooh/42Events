42 Events

See 42 Intra event times in your browser's local timezone and prepare a Google Calendar event.

Open 42 Intra
  1. Sign in with your existing 42 account.
  2. Refresh your profile and view the event list.
  3. Open an event, then select the calendar button.
  4. Review the details in Google Calendar and save if you wish.

The extension works on 42 Intra pages. It does not create calendar entries automatically or access your Google account.
